EXCLAIM Executive Admin Dashboard

Supported the rollout of a nationwide VA program by designing custom data entry, storage, and analytics solutions to help improve cancer treatment outcomes through the use of genetic testing and more targeted treatment selection.

This dashboard suite was designed to support executive visibility, operational tracking, and implementation monitoring across multiple views, including enterprise metrics, hiring tracker, VISN overview, facility profiles, personnel profiles, clinical workflows, and underlying data structure.
