VA - OIT - Customer Experience Survey

A Power BI and Power Automate reporting solution developed to distribute large-scale employee IT experience surveys across the Department of Veterans Affairs and deliver leadership-ready insights from monthly and annual feedback data.

Back to Projects

Description

For this project our team developed, distributed, and analyzed surveys capturing employees’ experiences and feedback on information technology services on monthly and an annual basis. Monthly survey participation requests were sent to one-twelfth of the entire Department of Veterans Affairs workforce each month, and employees were never solicited twice in a given year. Requests to participate in the annual survey were sent to the entire 500,000+ employee VA workforce at the end of the fiscal year, and each recipient received two follow-up reminders if they had not yet completed the survey. The distribution of the annual survey far exceeded the VA's daily outgoing email limits, so I created a highly scalable email automation process using Power Automate, which reduced the workload from eight employees working five hours daily to a single employee working 1.5 hours daily. Data from the surveys was visualized in a Power BI dashboard, where it was distributed to management to guide decision making.
