VA - VHA - NTO Service Expansion

A Power BI, Power Apps, SharePoint, and Power Automate solution developed to support the National Oncology Program’s TeleOncology service expansion by tracking geographic rollout, service maturity, and site onboarding progress.

Back to Projects

Description

This project supported the National Oncology Program’s expansion of TeleOncology services across the Veterans Health Administration. The program’s growth had two dimensions: expanding services geographically to new locations, sometimes in limited form, and maturing existing locations toward full implementation of the complete TeleOncology service offering. To support this effort, we first developed a Power BI dashboard that mapped participating sites, highlighted where services were available, and visually distinguished locations by the scope of services offered. As the program matured, the client needed a more robust operational tool to manage rollout progress, monitor onboarding, and support broader implementation tracking.

To meet that need, we adapted the foundation of the NOP Executive Action Tracker into a new solution tailored to this use case, leveraging SharePoint, Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI. The planned solution included a customized Power Apps canvas app and a dedicated SharePoint page to support site onboarding and service expansion management. A key challenge in this project was working closely with non-technical clinical leadership, who needed guidance not only on how the tool could be built, but also on what was feasible using existing Microsoft 365 tools without incurring additional costs. Development was progressing toward a mature implementation when the program was cut by DOGE in 2025.
